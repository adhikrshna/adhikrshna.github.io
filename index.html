From button-mashing
to shipping builds.

Games are what first made me wonder how things work under the hood. That curiosity turned into a B.Tech in Computer Science (AI & Data Analytics), and then into a job building software at Mindera — where I went from intern to full-time engineer.

At Mindera I owned an internal product end-to-end: gathering requirements, prioritising what ships next, building it across the stack, and getting it deployed. Along the way I found I enjoy the problem-definition side as much as the code — which is why I'm looking at product roles as well as engineering ones.
